Hi, I’m Alexandra. My mission is to help you unite your logical mind with your intuitive heart so they can work together — helping you feel alive in your body and in your life.
I found this work through my own challenges in academia. I found it odd that there’s no official course on how to intentionally create a happy life one desires, yet there are entire majors dedicated to building complicated machines.
As a mechanical engineer, my mission was to develop assistive technology to help people live healthier, happier lives. Over time, I realized the most powerful technology isn’t external. It’s internal.
Now, my work is about helping you learn to work with your own technology — your thoughts, emotions, physical body, and, at the root of it all, your personal energy — so that you can move through life with more ease, clarity, and joy.

#Personal Energy Coaching

This is a one-to-one coaching container designed to help high-performing professionals understand their energy and work with it intentionally to reduce burnout and move through life with greater ease and joy.

**Session**
- 10 individual coaching sessions
- Sessions are guided by your current goals, rather than a fixed curriculum

**Core Focus**
- Energy boosting practices to reduce chronic overload and stress
- Coaching questions that bring awareness to how your internal state (body, emotions, attention) interacts with your external environment
- Practical energy tools and techniques that integrate easily in your daily life

**You will be guided to hone…**
- Ability to increase energy quickly and easily
- Awareness of what fuels vs. drains your energy
